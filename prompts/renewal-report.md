## Instructions

Generate a renewal briefing with:

1. **Executive Summary** (2-3 sentences) — Overall health and renewal outlook
2. **Highlights** — What's going well (3-5 bullet points with specific data)
3. **Concerns** — What needs attention (be specific about declining trends)
4. **Tier Recommendation** — Stay same, upgrade (which tier and why), or downgrade risk
5. **Talking Points** — 5 conversation starters ranked by strategic value
6. **Recommended Approach** — How to frame the renewal conversation

Keep the entire report under 500 words. Use data, not opinions. Every claim should reference a specific number or event.
